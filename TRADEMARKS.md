# HearthVTT Trademark Policy

This is a short, plain-language statement of how the HearthVTT name and identity may be used. It is not a contract. As the project grows, this document may be replaced with something more formal; until then, please treat the spirit of this policy as the rule.

---

## What is covered

The following are considered the **HearthVTT identity**, and are not licensed under the AGPL:

- The name **"HearthVTT"** (and confusingly similar variants)
- The HearthVTT logo, wordmark, and other visual branding
- Project iconography, color schemes, and trade dress used to identify HearthVTT specifically

These are reserved to protect the project's identity from being used to misrepresent unrelated software, modifications, or services as official HearthVTT.

## What is not covered

The **source code, documentation, and design artifacts** of HearthVTT are licensed under [`AGPL-3.0-or-later`](LICENSE). The trademark policy in this document does **not** restrict any of the rights granted by that license. In particular:

- You may fork the code.
- You may modify it.
- You may run modified versions, including as a network service (subject to the AGPL).
- You may redistribute the code and your modifications, under AGPL-3.0-or-later.

You just have to do so under a **different name and branding** if your version is materially modified or could be confused with the official project.

## What you can do without asking

- Refer to HearthVTT by name in articles, blog posts, videos, talks, social media, academic papers, and similar uses.
- Link to the project's repository or website.
- Say truthful things like _"compatible with HearthVTT"_, _"a plugin for HearthVTT"_, or _"fork of HearthVTT"_ (the last one only if it is, in fact, a fork — and only as a description, not as your project's name).
- Use unmodified screenshots of the project in reviews, tutorials, and similar materials.
- Distribute **unmodified** builds of HearthVTT under the HearthVTT name.

## What you should not do without permission

- Use "HearthVTT" (or a confusingly similar name) as the name of a fork, modified distribution, hosted service, app, or product.
- Use the HearthVTT logo or visual branding for your fork, service, or product.
- Imply official endorsement, partnership, or affiliation with the HearthVTT project where none exists.
- Register domain names, social media handles, package names, or trademarks that incorporate "HearthVTT" in a way that could mislead users.

If you maintain a fork or run a modified hosted version, please pick your own name and visual identity. Truthful "based on HearthVTT" / "fork of HearthVTT" language in descriptions is fine.

## Why this exists

HearthVTT is meant to stay community-owned. The AGPL protects the code; this trademark policy protects users from being misled about _which_ software they're running and _who_ stands behind it. A fork is welcome to compete on the merits; it just needs to compete under its own name.

## Getting in touch

For trademark questions or permission requests, open an issue in the project's repository and tag it `trademark`. As the project formalizes, a dedicated contact will replace this.
